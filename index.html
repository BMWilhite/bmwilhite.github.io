01 / Business operationsReal business case study

Prestige Cleaning:
Operations Transformation

Building a more predictable recurring residential service line in an established Tucson business.

Prestige Cleaning Arizona LLC served Realtors, property managers, and commercial customers. One-time projects often brought unpredictable labor needs. I led an August–October 2026 initiative to develop recurring service and anticipate growth bottlenecks.

Acquisition
Shifted marketing toward recurring residential customers, using specialized marketing assistance while retaining ownership of advertising assets.
Hiring & training
Introduced group and individual interviews, followed by approximately three-day paid working interviews. Simplified routine-cleaning training to about one week; complex deep-cleaning work previously required 2–3 months.
Technology
Moved from Airtable/ClickUp toward BookingKoala, prioritizing a usable estimating calculator and automated customer communications.
Administration
Developed smaller LLM-assisted workflows for invoicing and operational checklists.

Operating priority: Frequent field work meant processes needed to reduce dependence on my constant availability before pursuing more customer volume.

03 / AI & decision supportCompleted MVP

JobOps Agent Network

A local workspace for discovering opportunities, screening qualifications, and preparing application materials.

The problem
Opportunity research, qualification review, and application preparation need a consistent workflow with evidence behind candidate claims.
My contribution
I designed the workflow and developed the MVP with substantial AI-assisted coding, alongside hands-on testing, debugging, and iteration.
What it delivers
Job discovery, a local dashboard, structured qualification screening, candidate-evidence validation and traceability, editable Word exports, and feedback and review features.
Why it matters for operations
A working example of turning an ambiguous process into structured steps, review checkpoints, and usable outputs.

Human control: Approval gates before paid AI operations. Applications are not submitted automatically.
